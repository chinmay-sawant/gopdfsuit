A · One PDF-reader seam for all ops (ops walk)

Today every op parses PDFs its own way. The shared xref/ module is write-only. A xref/object-stream fix must land in 3+ places.

Top recommendation

Tackle A1 (one PDF-reader seam) first. It has the most leverage: unifying object/xref-stream reading unblocks A2 (inflation moves with it), A4 (encryption consumes the same object view), and A3 (field-locate tests through the new seam). Migrate compress first — smallest consumer — then redact, form, merge.

Second: B1+B2 (gopdflib owns its types and validation) — gates every future cross-tier change. Third: E2 (schema golden test) — cheap, prevents silent drift while the bigger deepens land.
